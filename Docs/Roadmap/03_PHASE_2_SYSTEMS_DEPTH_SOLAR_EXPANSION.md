# Phase 2 – Systems Depth & Solar Expansion

**Status:** After Phase 1  
**Duration:** 8–12 weeks  
**Goal:** Transform the polished three-body demo into a true multi-body strategy sandbox. Players should feel real strategic depth when expanding across the Solar System, with body-specific constraints, deeper economy, and meaningful tech choices.

---

## Primary Objectives

1. Expand the celestial body catalog beyond Earth / Luna / Mars.
2. Deepen the economic and logistical systems so expansion has real cost and reward.
3. Make each body feel unique in resources, threats, base constraints, and specialist behavior.
4. Grow the tech tree into a proper Alpha Centauri-style decision space.
5. Support multi-campus / forward-base play with logistical tension.

---

## New Celestial Bodies (Priority Order)

| Body | Theme | Key Resources | Constraints & Threats | Base-Building Flavor |
|------|-------|---------------|-----------------------|----------------------|
| **Asteroid Belt** | Resource rush, low gravity | Metals, rare minerals, ice | Swarm threats, micro-gravity movement, limited flat surfaces | Modular tethered habitats, mining outposts |
| **Europa / Ganymede** | Ice & subsurface | Water ice, possible organics | Radiation, ice crust, subsurface pressure | Subsurface or insulated modules, radiation shielding |
| **Titan** | Thick atmosphere, hydrocarbons | Methane, organics, nitrogen | Dense air, cold, unique chemistry | Atmospheric-adapted habitats, different power solutions |
| **Outer System** (optional stretch) | Extreme distance | Helium-3, exotic ices | Long resupply times, isolation | Self-sufficient closed-loop bases |

Each body must change:
- What buildings are efficient or even possible
- What specialists prefer to do
- What threats appear and how they behave
- Resource extraction yields and logistics cost back to Earth or other bases

---

## Systems to Deepen

### 1. Economy & Logistics (AoE2 + SMAC)
- Local extraction chains with drop-off efficiency and saturation.
- Housing / population / power pressure that forces expansion or specialization.
- Earth resupply fleets as strategic events (timing, cost, risk).
- Inter-body logistics cost (shipping resources or specialists between campuses).
- Forward outposts with higher upkeep but access to unique nodes.

### 2. Multi-Campus Play
- Multiple simultaneous campuses with independent specialist pools or limited sharing.
- Claim and defend secondary sites.
- Body-specific NavMesh and movement rules (low-g, radiation zones, etc.).

### 3. Tech Tree Expansion (Alpha Centauri spirit)
- Branching choices: pure extraction vs terraforming vs defense doctrine.
- Tech that unlocks new building types, specialist upgrades, flag options, or body access.
- Secret Project / Wonder milestones that require dedicated focus and grant permanent bonuses.
- Research that subtly alters specialist personality weights or greed thresholds.

### 4. Specialist Guilds & Housing
- Guild / hall buildings that attract and house higher-tier specialists.
- Upgradable housing that improves rest recovery or unlocks party bonuses.
- Specialist “reputation” or loyalty that slowly improves with successful high-bounty work.

### 5. Threat Ecology
- Body-native fauna that responds to player expansion (not pure timed waves).
- Adaptive behaviors: more aggressive near high-value extractors, retreat when cleared, etc.
- Clear counters via specialist composition + flag types.

### 6. Flag System Depth
- Additional flag types (e.g., Terraform, Escort, Establish Outpost, Research Site).
- Dynamic risk based on local threat pressure.
- Shared reward pools and visible claim competition between specialists.

---

## Success Metrics

- Players can meaningfully choose different expansion paths (resource rush vs secure stepwise colonization).
- Each new body feels distinct in the first 10 minutes of play.
- Expanding to a second or third campus creates real logistical and specialist allocation decisions.
- Tech choices create noticeable differences in how the late game plays.
- The “I am still the Overseer” fantasy remains intact even as the solar system grows.

---

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Too many bodies dilute focus | Prioritize Belt + Europa first; Titan as stretch goal |
| Logistics complexity overwhelms | Keep inter-body shipping simple and event-driven at first |
| Tech tree becomes overwhelming | Cap early branches; use Secret Projects as clear milestones |
| Specialist pool management becomes micro | Limit simultaneous active specialists or use strong party tools |

---

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] At least two new bodies fully playable with unique rules
- [ ] Multi-campus logistics functional
- [ ] Expanded tech tree with at least two meaningful branches + Secret Projects
- [ ] 5–6 specialist classes with clear personalities
- [ ] Economy and threat systems support the larger scale
- [ ] Ready for content explosion without further core system rewrites
