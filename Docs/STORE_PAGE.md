## About this game

**Govern by incentive, not by clicks.** In Solar Majesty you never select a unit and order it
around. You are the Overseer: you build the colony, set the economy, and post bounties on the map.
Your robots are free agents. Each weighs the reward against the danger, the distance, how tired it
is and how brave it feels. A cheap bounty on a dangerous den gets ignored. Raise the price and see
who bites.

**Five worlds, one campaign.** Start on Earth, then launch for the Moon, Mars, the Asteroid Belt and
Europa. Each world has its own terrain, creatures and pressure: soil creepers chewing farms on
Earth, dust wisps draining power on Mars, rock mites swarming the Belt's mines, fissure leeches under
Europa's ice. Hold the population target, research the next launch, and move on. Your stockpile and
research travel with you.

**Robots with a service record.** Workshops fabricate ten classes, from Scout drones and Engineers
to Defense mechs, Medics, Geologists, Harvesters, Surveyors, Couriers, Sentinels and Terraformers.
Every robot is named, levels up, buys its own gear from your guild halls and market, and remembers
the contracts it took. Lose one and the Fobot Yard will rebuild it, for a price.

**An economy that walks.** Taxes don't teleport into your treasury. A courier has to carry the levy
home, and a mite can steal it on the way. Guild halls, a market, a blacksmith and watchtowers give
your credits somewhere to go and your robots something to want.

**Play it your way.** Choose a doctrine (Open Hands, Aegis Watch or Survey First), add a challenge
(Austere, Swarm or Tight Purse), switch to Endless, or turn on Ironman. Twelve achievements reward
different styles of play rather than grinding.

**Optional: robots that talk back.** Turn on *Hero voices · local AI* and your robots speak in their
own voices and answer when you message them. The model runs entirely on your PC; nothing is sent
online. It is off by default and the game is complete without it.
